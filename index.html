Why we started

Built by students who refused to look away.

Annadatri began with a simple belief: young people don’t need to wait to make a difference. Founded by Anusha, the initiative connects immediate food support with long-term nutrition awareness.

  1. Respond nowNutritious meals and food packages for communities that need them.
  2. Teach for tomorrowPractical nutrition education that works with real budgets and local food.
  3. Grow togetherStudents, local partners and communities working side by side.
